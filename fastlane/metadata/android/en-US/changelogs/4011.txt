KeliBoard now uses its own app identity and can coexist with HeliBoard.

- New rounded-keycap KeliBoard icon
- Complete Korean settings translation
- First-class Korean and Cheonjiin input fixes
- Fixed Hangul input changing the word ending after moving the cursor into a word
- Lock-screen QWERTY and punctuation fixes
- Stability and long-press improvements from the KeliBoard preview series

Android cannot migrate data between app identities. Back up settings in an earlier preview and restore them in this version.
