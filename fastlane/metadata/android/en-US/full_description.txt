KeliBoard is a modern, actively maintained HeliBoard fork focused on stability, usability, and first-class language support.

It is privacy-conscious, fully open source, and works offline without the internet permission.

Highlights:
<ul>
  <li>Fixes for long-standing keyboard, composition, emoji, and spacing issues</li>
  <li>First-class Korean support, including Cheonjiin and Korean QWERTY layouts</li>
  <li>Customizable layouts, themes, colors, icons, fonts, and background images</li>
  <li>Dictionaries for suggestions and spell checking</li>
  <li>Multilingual typing</li>
  <li>Clipboard history</li>
  <li>One-handed and split keyboard modes</li>
  <li>Number pad</li>
  <li>Backup and restore for settings and learned data</li>
</ul>

Korean is a flagship feature, not the limit of the project. KeliBoard remains a general-purpose keyboard for HeliBoard users in every language.

KeliBoard is based on HeliBoard, which is based on AOSP and OpenBoard. Upstream attribution and licenses are preserved.
